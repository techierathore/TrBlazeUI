  • Fan-out is unobserved on 10 of the 16 runs in the two phases that fan out.
  • Fourteen gate records are false (TF-001 replays) and cannot be withdrawn. The six false misses now can be and are. A record that withdraws a gate record, in the shape of the existing miss-void, would let §2 and §3 stop carrying this note.
